## week 2

deep dive into [[learning]] theory. the feynman technique is genuinely underrated — most of what I thought I understood collapsed under the requirement to explain it simply.

::: branch a tangent on note-taking systems
I've tried zettelkasten, cornell notes, bullet journaling, plain text dumps. what actually works for me: writing notes in my own words immediately after reading, without looking at the source.

this forces active recall during the capture phase itself. the note is already a review.

the format matters less than the habit.
:::
